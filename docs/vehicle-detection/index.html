1. Scene routingBrightness/value statistics choose the daytime or nighttime path.
2. Daytime previewMotion foreground regions are extracted against a slowly updated background model.
3. Nighttime previewBright components are isolated to approximate the original headlight-oriented branch.
4. Vehicle regionsConnected regions are filtered and displayed as candidate vehicle boxes inside the road ROI.
The browser preview is a lightweight client-side approximation; adjacent vehicles may merge and some vehicles may be missed. The original MATLAB source contains the full GMM/morphology/component pipeline.
